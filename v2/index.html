How HexTV Works

No sign-ups. Watch in your browser or the app — pick something and you're in a room in seconds.

01

Pick Something To Watch

Browse the library of movies and series and pick a season and episode — or paste a YouTube link into a room.

02

Invite Your Friends

Hit Watch, then Invite, and share the room link. It opens straight in the HexTV app if they have it — no account needed.

03

Press Play Together

Play, pause and seek stay in sync for everyone. On a series, the next episode starts for the whole room.

04

Chat While You Watch

React, laugh, and discuss the movie in the live chat panel — right next to the video.
